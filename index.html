What I did

  • Where it started. The original website presented a lot of information at once. Bold colors, crowded layouts, and inconsistent styling made it difficult to establish a clear hierarchy.
  • Building the user journey. I planned each redesign around clearer navigation, stronger content hierarchy, and the company's expanding services. The biggest shift was moving from adding more content to making existing content easier to navigate.

Sip Sober

I created Sip Sober, a consumer iOS app for discovering zero-proof drinks and venues in New York City. I led the product from concept through design and development, building the experience in SwiftUI and Firebase while continually refining its structure, features, and visual system.

Founder · Product design · iOS — SwiftUI, Firebase · sipsober.app ↗

100+ NYC venues 5.0 ★ App Store rating Live on the App Store

How it came together

  • From prototype to iOS app. Sip Sober began as a simple web-based map. It validated the core discovery experience, but revealed an opportunity for a native app where users could explore venues, filter results, and save places.
  • Rethinking the app structure. Home evolved into Discover, a dedicated space for exploring venues and curated guides. Favorites expanded into a broader Profile with saved places, while the Map remained central to discovery.
  • A stronger visual identity. I introduced the dark navy interface and updated the cards, navigation, typography, and selected states so Discover, Map, and Profile felt like parts of the same app.
