01 · Website redesign

MyAbroadMDs

I manage the design, development and ongoing updates of this healthcare company's WordPress site. As the business grew, I took it from a dense template to custom, responsive pages, making existing content easier to navigate instead of adding more.

Role
Design, development & marketing
Platform

Focus
UX, ecommerce, SEO

Simpler with every iteration

Custom and responsive

HTML/CSS sections built inside WordPress that work across desktop, tablet and mobile.

See the full process

01 The first redesign

A consistent color palette, typography, imagery and navigation created a foundation for what came next.

02 Planning the structure

I planned the next phase around clearer navigation, stronger content hierarchy and the company's expanding services.

03 Simplifying the user journey

The biggest shift was moving from adding more content to making existing content easier to navigate.

04 Going beyond the template

Custom HTML and CSS sections gave me more control over the design and kept the experience consistent across new services.

05 Designed across devices

Layouts adapted for tablet and mobile, keeping content readable and navigation consistent.

The result A more flexible website I can keep developing as the business grows.

02 · iOS app · Founder

Sip Sober

A consumer iOS app for discovering zero-proof drinks and venues in New York City. I led it from concept through design and development, building it in SwiftUI and Firebase, from a browser map prototype to a native app.

Role
Product, UX & iOS development
Built with
SwiftUI, Firebase
Focus
Discovery, navigation, visual identity
Discover

More ways to find drinks

Home became Discover, with drink categories and curated guides beyond the map.

Map

Easier exploring

Filters, venue details and map interactions make it easier to narrow down where to go.

Profile

Saved in one place

Favorites grew into Profile, bringing saved venues and account features together.

See the full process

01 From prototype to iOS app

Sip Sober began as a browser-based map. It validated the core idea and showed the need for a native app with filters, saved places and discovery beyond the map.

02 Rethinking the app structure

Home evolved into Discover, Favorites expanded into Profile with saved places, and the Map stayed central.

03 A stronger visual identity

A dark navy interface with updated cards, navigation, typography and selected states made Discover, Map and Profile feel like one app.
