I’m a digital marketing and web specialist with experience across website design, development, UX, content strategy, ecommerce, and SEO. This portfolio highlights two projects: the ongoing evolution of the MyAbroadMDs website and Sip Sober, a consumer iOS app I created and built from concept through development.

01 / MYABROADMDS

A website that evolved with the business

The redesign was not a single before-and-after project. As MyAbroadMDs expanded its services, I continued revisiting the website to improve how information was organized, how users moved through the experience, and how the brand was communicated visually.

ORIGINAL WEBSITE

Limited visual hierarchy

The dominant royal blue and dense layout made important information compete for attention.

Inconsistent brand experience

Imagery, typography, and page structure lacked a cohesive visual system.

Unclear user journey

With many services presented at once, visitors had few visual cues guiding them toward the most relevant next step.

Establishing a clearer direction

The first redesign focused on creating a cleaner visual system and stronger hierarchy. I refined the color palette, simplified the navigation and hero, and introduced more consistent imagery and typography. These early iterations established a stronger visual direction while revealing where the user journey still needed work.

FIRST REDESIGN Refined palette, simplified navigation, and stronger visual hierarchy.

02 / MYABROADMDS

Planning the redesign

After the first redesign established a stronger visual direction, I began planning the next evolution of the website. I mapped out page layouts, content priorities, and design changes before rebuilding, with a greater focus on how users would move through the site and understand the company’s growing services.

03 / MYABROADMDS

Building the user journey

As MyAbroadMDs added more services and content, I shifted from designing individual sections to thinking about how visitors moved through the homepage as a whole. The goal became creating clearer pathways to key services while making a growing amount of information easier to navigate.

EARLY ITERATION
EXPANDING THE EXPERIENCE
REFINING THE JOURNEY

Added cleaner entry points

Membership, quiz, video, and service content gave visitors more obvious next steps.

Reorganized dense information

Long blocks of copy became more distinct content areas that could be scanned more quickly.

Simplified as the site grew

As the page expanded, I began simplifying and prioritizing rather than continuing to add sections.
